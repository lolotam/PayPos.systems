### Voice
- **Arabic-first**, Gulf-friendly Modern Standard Arabic; English is a full equal, never a machine translation.
- Short, concrete, owner-to-owner: *"Close the shift in one tap."* not *"Leverage seamless shift management."*
- Numbers are exact and formatted: `12.500 KWD` / `12.500 د.ك` (always three decimals, tabular figures).

## 2. Colour

### 2.1 Strategy
Payments and POS in the region are **bank blue** (KNET, MyFatoorah, banks) and **purple** (Foodics, Fresha).
PosPay owns **Ember**, a warm signature orange that is rare in the category. It nods to the first tenants'
smokehouse and grill and to hospitality in general, and is anchored by a near-black **Souq Ink** that carries the
trust money needs. **Gulf Teal** means *paid / positive* and nothing else, and **Pearl Sand** warms the white space and
recalls Kuwait's pearl-diving heritage.

### 2.2 Core palette

| Swatch | Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK | Pantone (approx.) | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 🟧 | **Ember** · جمرة | `#FF6421` | 255 100 33 | 0 61 87 0 | ≈ 165 C | Signature. Logo, primary CTAs, active nav, focus. ~10–15 % of a layout. |
| ⬛ | **Souq Ink** · حبر السوق | `#0D1522` | 13 21 34 | 62 38 0 87 | ≈ Black 6 C | Anchor. Type, one-colour logo, sidebars, dark-mode base. |
| 🟩 | **Gulf Teal** · فيروز الخليج | `#179F8E` | 23 159 142 | 86 0 11 38 | ≈ 3285 C | Paid / success / positive deltas only. |
| ⬜ | **Pearl Sand** · رمل اللؤلؤ | `#F7F1E8` | 247 241 232 | 0 2 6 3 | ≈ 9224 C | Warm neutral backgrounds, landing sections, e-menu. |

Pantone values are approximations. Verify against a physical guide before print. Ember is a vivid orange that can
print slightly duller in CMYK, so use the spot colour for signage and premium print.

**Usage ratio on a typical screen:** Sand/white 55 % · Ink 25 % · Ember 12 % · Teal 8 %.
Ember is a seasoning. If more than about a fifth of a screen is orange, it is wrong.

### 2.3 Scales
Full 50–950 scales for `ember`, `ink`, `teal` and `sand`, plus `amber`, `crimson` and `indigo` (semantic and chart
use only), are in `tokens/tokens.json` and exposed as Tailwind utilities (`bg-ember-500`, `text-ink-600`, …).
Visual sheet: `palette.png`.

### 2.4 Rules
- **Ember fill + Ink text**, never white text on Ember-500. White on Ember-500 fails contrast (2.96:1). For white
  text, use `brand-strong` (Ember-700 `#C0380A`, 5.51:1).
- Links on light backgrounds are Ember-700, not Ember-500.
- **Teal is only for "good" money states** (paid, received, profit up). Never decorate with it.
- Do not use Ember for errors. Errors are **Crimson** (`#AB1E3B`), which has a clearly different hue.
- One-colour logo: Souq Ink on light backgrounds, white on Ink or Ember.
- Never set the logo on a busy photo without an Ink or Sand plate behind it.

## 3. Typography

| Use | Typeface | Weights |
|---|---|---|
| Everything (Arabic + Latin) | **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** (already in `packages/ui`; free, OFL) | 400 · 500 · 700 |
| Latin fallback | IBM Plex Sans → system-ui | — |

- One family for both scripts keeps RTL and LTR screens visually identical. Arabic is the default and `:lang(en)`
  switches to Latin.
- Money uses `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` (already set on `body` in `theme.css`) so columns of KWD align.
- Type scale for apps (rem): 0.75 · 0.875 · 1 · 1.125 · 1.25 · 1.5 · 2 · 2.5. Landing hero: 3–4.5 rem, weight 700.
- The final wordmark will be custom-drawn outlines. Do not ship the logo as live text.

## 4. Applying the system — per surface

The shared CSS is `tokens/theme.css`, a **drop-in replacement** for `pospay/packages/ui/src/styles/theme.css`. It
keeps every existing variable name (`--primary`, `--paid`, `--open-shift` …) and adds `--brand`, `--brand-strong`,
`--link`, `--sidebar*`, `--success/warning/info` and `--chart-1…6`.

### 4.1 Landing page — `pospay.systems`
- Page `sand-50`; alternating sections `sand-100`; one full-bleed **Ink** band (hero or the "one system" story).
- Primary CTA: Ember fill, Ink text, `radius-control`. Secondary: Ink outline.
- Headlines in Ink, 700. Eyebrow labels in Ember-700, uppercase Latin or bold Arabic.
- Product screenshots sit on Ink cards with a thin `ink-800` border. In light sections use a soft `sand-200` border.
- Illustrations, if any, are flat, in Ember, Ink and Sand only, with the Sadu triangle motif as an optional divider.

### 4.2 Admin dashboard — `app.pospay.systems`
- Layout: **Ink sidebar** (`--sidebar`), with the active item as an Ember pill with Ink text. Content `sand-50`, cards white.
- `--primary` is **Ink**, for neutral heavy actions (Save, Approve). `--brand` is **Ember**, for the one hero action per
  screen (New sale, Approve commission statement).
- KPIs: number in Ink, positive delta in `--success` (teal), negative in `--destructive` (crimson), with neutral arrows.
- Charts: series order `--chart-1…6` (Ember, Teal, Indigo, Amber, Crimson, Slate). Ember = "this business / current
  period"; slate = "comparison / last period". Gridlines `ink-100`. No gradients and no 3D.
- Dark mode: `.dark` tokens (Ink-950 page, Ink-900 cards, Ember-400 accents). All pairs are contrast-checked.

### 4.3 POS web app — `pos.pospay.systems`
- Touch-first. **Pay / Charge** is the only Ember button on the sell screen, full-width, Ink text, at least 56 px tall.
- Order states use the domain tokens: `paid` teal · `pending` amber · `failed` crimson · `low-stock` ember-800 ·
  `open-shift` indigo, always with an icon and label, never colour alone.
- Offer a dark theme for low-light restaurants and the kitchen KDS. On KDS, ticket age escalates neutral → amber → crimson.

### 4.4 E-menu & booking — `menu.pospay.systems`
- This is the tenant's surface. Their logo and accent replace `--brand` only. Layout, type, Ink and Sand stay PosPay.
- A small "Powered by PosPay" mark sits in the footer in one-colour Ink.

### 4.5 Receipts (ESC/POS, thermal)
- One-colour black. Use the symbol-only logo, at least 12 mm wide. Arabic shaping is applied before printing.

## 5. Logo (status: choosing)
20 concepts were generated with the logo-design skill's method (brief → clichés → word map → mark types →
concepts). See `../logos/LOGO-CONCEPTS.md` and `../logos/contact-sheet.png`. Once a direction is chosen, the next
steps are to redraw it as clean SVG (the generated images are raster concept art, not production masters), then build
the kit: one-colour and reversed versions, horizontal and stacked lockups, a 16 px cut, favicon, app icon, web
manifest and clear-space rules.

**Logo rules that already apply:** minimum clear space = the height of the P's bowl; minimum size 16 px
(symbol) / 80 px (horizontal lockup); approved backgrounds are white, Sand, Ink and Ember (symbol in Ink).
